How It Works

Here are the steps we'll follow, from first call to organized space.

1

Free Consultation

20-minute Call over Google Meet

We'll discuss your space, what's working, what's not, and what you're hoping to accomplish. I'll explain how I work and answer any questions.

By the end, you'll have a clear sense of how we'd work together and what's possible for your space, with no pressure to commit.

Book a Free Consult
2

Understand Your Space

When we meet in your home, we'll walk through the space together and I'll ask lots of questions about how you use it.

I want to understand your routines, your biggest pain points, and what's getting in your way. With this information, I can create a system that's intuitive, easy to maintain, and built around the way you live.

3

Sort & Declutter

We'll group similar items together into categories so we can see exactly what you have.

Then we'll decide what to:

  • Keep
  • Donate
  • Relocate
  • Recycle
  • Toss

You'll always make the final call on your belongings.

4

Design Your System

Once we know what's staying, I'll design a layout that makes sense for your home and your routines.

Frequently used items stay within easy reach.
Occasional-use items get stored thoughtfully.
Everything gets an intentional home.

5

Product Sourcing (if needed)

Only after we've decluttered do I recommend organizing products.

I'll take measurements, research the best options, and send you a simple shopping list for approval. Once approved, I can purchase everything for you so you don't have to.

6

Put Everything in Place

I'll organize everything into its new home using simple, functional systems.

Labels are added whenever they'll make life easier for you or your family.

7

Final Walkthrough

We'll walk through the finished space together and make any final adjustments. I'll show you where everything lives, so you feel confident keeping it up long after our session ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost?

I offer full-day, 6-hour sessions and charge $85/hour.

If we decide your space needs organizing products and I purchase them for you, you'll reimburse me for the cost of the products, plus any transportation costs.

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Prospect Heights, Brooklyn and primarily work with clients throughout Brooklyn and nearby neighborhoods in Manhattan and Queens.

Do I need to be there the whole time?

Not the whole time, but I will need your input throughout the project.

We'll work together to decide what to keep, design your new system, and review any product recommendations. In between, many clients choose to work from home, take meetings, run errands, or spend time with their family while I continue organizing.

Do you work with what I already have?

Absolutely.

Buying containers is never the goal. Creating a system that works for you is.

I'll always start by using the containers, baskets, furniture, and other organizing products you already own. If your space would benefit from something new, I'll recommend it, but only if it makes your space more functional. The decision is always yours.

What if my space needs new organizing products?

I don't purchase organizing products before our first session.

We'll first sort through everything and create a plan for your space. Then I'll take measurements, research the right options, and create a list of product recommendations.

From there, you can order the products yourself, or I can purchase them on your behalf.
