How it works

Every project follows the same path, from the first call to the final walkthrough. Here it is in full — grouped into four phases so you can see the shape of it.

Phase 1 — Consult

1

Free consultation

20-minute call over Google Meet

We’ll talk through your space, what’s working, what isn’t, and what you’re hoping to accomplish. I’ll explain how I work and answer your questions. By the end you’ll have a clear sense of what’s possible — with no pressure to commit.

Book a consult

Phase 2 — Understand & edit

2

Understand your space

When we meet in your home, we walk through it together and I ask a lot of questions about how you actually use it — your routines, your pain points, what keeps getting in your way. That’s what a good system gets built around.

3

Sort & declutter

We group similar items into categories so you can see everything you have. Then we decide what to:

  • Keep
  • Donate
  • Relocate
  • Recycle
  • Toss

You always make the final call on your belongings.

Phase 3 — Design & source

4

Design your system

Once we know what’s staying, I design a layout that fits your home and your routines. Frequently used things stay within easy reach, occasional-use items get stored thoughtfully, and everything gets an intentional home.

5

Source products — only if needed

Containers are never the starting point. Only after we’ve decluttered do I recommend products, and only where they genuinely help. I’ll measure, research options, and send a simple shopping list for approval — then handle the ordering so you don’t have to.

Phase 4 — Implement & review

6

Put everything in place

I set everything into its new home using simple, functional systems, and add labels wherever they’ll make life easier for you or your family.

7

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished space together and make any final adjustments. I’ll show you where everything lives, so you feel confident keeping it up long after the session ends.

Questions, answered

How much does it cost?+

I offer full-day, six-hour sessions at $85/hour. If we decide your space needs organizing products and I purchase them for you, you’ll reimburse me for the products plus any transportation costs.

What areas do you serve?+

I’m based in Prospect Heights, Brooklyn, and primarily work with clients throughout Brooklyn and nearby neighborhoods in Manhattan and Queens.

Do I need to be there the whole time?+

Not the whole time, but I will need your input along the way — deciding what to keep, shaping the new system, and reviewing any product recommendations. In between, many clients work from home, take meetings, run errands, or spend time with family while I keep going.

Do you work with what I already have?+

Absolutely. Buying containers is never the goal — a system that works for you is. I start with the baskets, bins, and furniture you already own, and only suggest something new if it genuinely makes the space more functional. The decision is always yours.

What if my space needs new products?+

I don’t buy anything before our first session. We sort through everything and make a plan first. Then I measure, research the right options, and put together a list — which you can order yourself, or I can purchase on your behalf.
